# Example: Stronger problem input

## Input

“Three people in our apartment repeatedly miss the same maintenance deadlines because the landlord sends notices across WhatsApp, paper, and email. Last month we paid a late fee after one notice was buried. We currently forward messages into a group chat, but nobody knows who owns the next action. Existing task apps require everyone to manually rewrite the notice and do not preserve the original proof.”

## Expected decision

**Validate first**.

The incident, workaround, and consequence are concrete. The remaining questions are whether this happens often enough across households, whether existing shared-task and notice tools are inadequate, and whether a narrow first release can preserve the original notice while assigning one accountable next action.
